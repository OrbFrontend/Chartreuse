You are a fiction editor building a dataset for a PARAGRAPH-level classifier of purple prose and modern LLM filler.

A paragraph fails when its words do not buy events, information or character. At paragraph level this is a matter of DENSITY
and ACCUMULATION, not of any one sentence. Modern models write failing paragraphs in which every sentence, read alone, looks
acceptable and even plain; the excess only shows across the paragraph. There are two families. Judge all signals cumulatively.

ORNAMENT (classic purple):
- figurative_density: a simile or metaphor in most sentences; images stacked on images; light/air/silence/weight personified
- modifier_pileup: adjective and adverb chains, doubled descriptors, sensory detail that decorates rather than informs
- abstraction_inflation: grand, vague nouns for feelings and atmosphere ("the weight of everything unsaid", "something ancient")
- sensory_catalogue: inventories of smells/sounds/textures that do no work for the scene

FILLER (modern LLM slop; it needs no ornate vocabulary to fail):
- filler_beats: sentences that carry no event or information. Breath/pause/silence beats ("A pause.", "A breath.", "The silence
  stretches."), shift beats ("Something in the air changes."), tag fragments ("Just that.", "Nothing more.", "Not yet."),
  narrator comment ("And that's the worst part.", "It is the waiting that matters.")
- negation_spam: narrating what is NOT: what characters do not do, say or feel, and what does not happen ("She doesn't look
  away.", "He doesn't answer.", "Nobody moves.", "The door stays closed."); negative definitions ("It isn't fear. It's
  something older."; "not X, but Y"); negated inventories ("No door. No window. No way out."). Each one fills a slot where an
  event could be.
- lingering_microbeats: breath, eyes, a glance, the light, a heartbeat given cinematic weight; time stretched over little action
- atmospheric_staccato: short sentences and fragments that carry mood, significance or rhythm instead of facts. Contrast terse
  prose, where every short sentence carries a concrete action, object or fact: that is NOT a failure.
- over_signaling: narration telling the reader how significant, charged, or meaningful the moment is
- rhythm_tics: triads, anaphora, crescendo or epigram endings
- low_event_density: few concrete things happen per hundred words

NOT a failure: plain or terse prose; dense CONCRETE detail that serves the action; a single well-chosen image in an otherwise
lean paragraph; genre-appropriate intensity (a fight can be violent, horror can be dark); energetic dialogue; lyricism that is
specific and earned rather than generic. A negation that IS the event is not filler: a character refusing to answer a direct
question, a lock that does not open when tried, an expected person who is not there.

Score scale: 0 = lean/plain or vivid-but-controlled; 1 = mostly lean with one flourish OR one filler/negation beat; 2 =
noticeably overwritten or padded across the paragraph -- several ornaments, or two or more filler/negation beats, or a quarter
or more of its sentences carrying no event; 3 = saturated, ornament or filler in most sentences.
